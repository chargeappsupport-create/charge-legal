PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: May 13, 2026

This Privacy Notice for Charge (doing business as Charge App), describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services").

About Charge: Charge is a personal energy and productivity tracking application. To provide our features—including the "energy battery" visualizer, "Daily Spark" reflections, and your private "Journal"—we collect and store authentication data and your personal logs securely via Firebase.

Questions or concerns? If you still have any questions, please contact us at chargeapp.support@gmail.com.

SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS

This summary provides key points from our Privacy Notice. We process your information to provide, improve, and administer our Services, specifically your account management and energy analytics. We do not process sensitive personal information or collect info from third parties other than your authentication provider.

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

We collect personal information that you disclose to us, including names, email addresses, and authentication data. We also automatically collect device data such as your mobile device ID, model, and manufacturer to maintain app security.

Google API

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy.

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

We process your information to manage your account, deliver energy distribution analytics, and solve potential technical issues. We only process your data when we have a valid legal reason to do so.

3. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION?

We share data with third-party vendors who perform services for us: Google Firebase (for authentication and cloud database storage) and Vercel (for support site hosting).

4. DO WE USE COOKIES AND OTHER TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES?

We use essential technical tokens and local storage to maintain your login session. For details, see our Cookie Notice.

5. HOW DO WE HANDLE YOUR SOCIAL LOGINS?

If you log in via Google, we receive profile information like your name and email address from the provider to create your Charge account.

6. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

We keep your personal information only for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this notice, typically for the duration you have an active account with us.

7. HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?

We aim to protect your personal information through a system of organizational and technical security measures via Google Cloud and Firebase infrastructure.

8. DO WE COLLECT INFORMATION FROM MINORS?

We do not knowingly collect data from or market to children under 18 years of age. By using the app, you represent you are at least 18.

9. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?

Depending on your location, you have rights to access or delete your data. You can withdraw consent or terminate your account at any time in the app settings or by contacting us.

10. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

We do not currently respond to Do-Not-Track ("DNT") signals as no uniform technology standard has been finalized for implementation.

11. DO UNITED STATES RESIDENTS HAVE SPECIFIC PRIVACY RIGHTS?

Residents of states like California and Virginia have specific rights to request access to and deletion of their personal information. We do not sell your personal data.

12. DO WE MAKE UPDATES TO THIS NOTICE?

Yes, we will update this notice as necessary to stay compliant with relevant laws. The updated version will be indicated by the date at the top.

13. HOW CAN YOU CONTACT US ABOUT THIS NOTICE?

If you have questions, you can email us at chargeapp.support@gmail.com.

14. HOW CAN YOU REVIEW, UPDATE, OR DELETE THE DATA WE COLLECT FROM YOU?

You have the right to request access to your data. To review or delete your information, please visit our Contact Page.
