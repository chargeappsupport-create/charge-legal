PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: May 13, 2026

This Privacy Notice for Charge (doing business as Charge App), describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you download and use our mobile application, Charge.

About Charge: Charge is a personal energy and productivity tracking application designed to help users manage their daily capacity and personal growth. The app allows users to authenticate via an email address and create a personal profile. Core features include tracking daily tasks (which visually fill a user's "energy battery"), answering daily reflection prompts ("Daily Spark"), and writing private text entries in a personal "Journal." All data is collected and processed strictly to provide a personalized, localized tracking and analytics experience.

Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at chargeapp.support@gmail.com.

SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS

What personal information do we process? We process information depending on how you interact with Charge. This includes your email for authentication and the task/energy data you input into the app.

Do we process sensitive info? No. We do not actively request sensitive personal information.

How do we keep your info safe? We use technical security measures via Google Cloud and Firebase to protect your data, though no system is 100% secure.

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

In Short: We collect personal information that you provide to us.

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products, or when you participate in activities on the Services. The personal information we collect includes:

  • Email addresses
  • Names
  • Passwords and authentication data

Sensitive Information (Journal Feature)

While we do not actively request sensitive personal information, users may voluntarily input personal details into free-text fields like the Journal. We do not use this free-text data for marketing or profiling, and we treat all user-generated content with strict security measures.

Application Data

If you use our application, we automatically collect device information such as your mobile device ID, model, and manufacturer, as well as operating system and system configuration information. This information is primarily needed to maintain the security and operation of Charge.
