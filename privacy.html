PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated May 9, 2026


This Privacy Notice for Charge (doing business as Charge App), describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including the mobile application Charge.
Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at chargeapp.support@gmail.com.

SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS

This summary provides key points from our Privacy Notice. You can find more details by using our table of contents below.
What personal information do we process? When you use Charge, we process information depending on your interactions. Charge tracks your tasks, daily energy states, and journal entries to provide personalized analytics. All data is processed strictly to provide a localized tracking experience.

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

We collect personal information you disclose to us, including email addresses, names, and contact/authentication data (via Google Auth). We also collect mobile device data (ID, model, OS) to maintain the security and operation of the application.

Google API

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy.

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

We process your information to facilitate account creation, deliver services (task tracking and energy distribution trends), and respond to inquiries. We aim to protect your personal information through a system of organizational and technical security measures.

3. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION?

We share data with third-party vendors who perform services for us, including Google Firebase (for authentication and data storage) and Vercel (for hosting). We do not sell your personal information.

4. DO WE USE COOKIES?

Yes, we use cookies and similar tracking technologies to maintain security and analyze usage. Specific information is available in our Cookie Notice.

5. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?

You can review, change, or terminate your account at any time. To request deletion of your data, please visit our Contact Page or use the account termination feature in the app settings.
