PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: May 13, 2026

This Privacy Notice for Charge (doing business as Charge App), describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you download and use our mobile application, Charge.

Charge is a personal energy and productivity tracking application designed to help users manage their daily capacity and personal growth. To function, the app collects and stores user account information (email), custom task lists, and personal journal entries. All data is processed strictly to provide a personalized, localized tracking and analytics experience.

Questions or concerns? If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions, please contact us at chargeapp.support@gmail.com.

SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS

What personal information do we process? We process information depending on how you interact with Charge. This includes your email for authentication and the task/energy data you input into the app.

Do we process sensitive info? No. We do not process sensitive personal information.

How do we keep your info safe? We use technical security measures via Google Cloud and Firebase to protect your data, though no system is 100% secure.

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

We collect personal information you disclose to us, including email addresses, names, and authentication data. When you use the app, we also automatically collect mobile device data (ID, model, and OS) to maintain the security and operation of the service.

Google API

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy.

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

We process your information to facilitate account creation, deliver energy distribution trends, and provide support. We may also process data to identify usage trends and evaluate app performance.

3. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION?

We share data with third-party vendors including Google Firebase (for authentication and cloud storage) and Vercel (for support site hosting). We do not sell your personal information.

4. DO WE USE COOKIES?

We use essential technical tokens to maintain your secure session. For details, see our Cookie Notice.

5. HOW DO WE HANDLE YOUR SOCIAL LOGINS?

If you log in using a third-party account (like Google Sign-In), we receive certain profile information like your name and email address to create your account.

6. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

We keep your information for as long as you have an active account with us, or as required by law for accounting or legal purposes.

7. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?

You can review, change, or terminate your account at any time. To request deletion of your data, visit our Contact Page.

8. DO UNITED STATES RESIDENTS HAVE SPECIFIC PRIVACY RIGHTS?

Residents of states like California, Colorado, and Virginia have specific rights to access and delete their data. We have not sold or shared any personal information with third parties for a commercial purpose in the preceding 12 months.
