COOKIE POLICY

Last updated May 13, 2026
This Cookie Policy explains how Charge ("Company," "we," "us," and "our") uses cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage and authentication tokens) to recognize you when you use our mobile application and visit our website at https://daily-charge-flow.vercel.app ("Website").

What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device. In the context of the Charge mobile app, we use Firebase Authentication tokens and local storage which act similarly to cookies to keep you signed into your account and secure your user session.

Strictly necessary technical identifiers:

These are essential for the app to function. They allow us to verify who you are and keep your energy logs and journal entries synced to your specific account.

Provider: Google Firebase

Service: Firebase Authentication

Purpose: Maintains secure user session and login status.

Analytics and performance technologies:

We use these to understand how users interact with Charge so we can improve the app and fix crashes.

Provider: Google Firebase

Service: Google Analytics for Firebase / Crashlytics

Purpose: Monitors app performance and tracks anonymous usage trends.
