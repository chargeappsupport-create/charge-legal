COOKIE POLICY

Last updated: May 13, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Charge App ("Company," "we," "us," and "our") uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you use our application and visit our website at https://daily-charge-flow.vercel.app ("Website"). It explains what these technologies are and why we use them, as well as your rights to control our use of them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device. In the context of a mobile app, similar technologies like Firebase Authentication tokens and Local Storage are used to maintain your secure session and identify your account.

Why do we use cookies?

We use essential technologies for several technical reasons in order for our Services to operate. These are "strictly necessary" to provide you with secure access to your account and energy logs.

Essential and Analytics Technologies

Name Provider / Service Purpose Type / Duration
__auth_token Google Firebase Used to securely identify your account (UID) and keep you logged in. Local Storage / Persistent
_crash_id Firebase Crashlytics Gathers anonymous data regarding app crashes and performance bugs. Session Identifier

How can I control cookies?

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. Essential technologies cannot be rejected as they are strictly necessary to provide you with account services. You can manage your preferences through your mobile device settings or by contacting us.

What about other tracking technologies?

We may use similar technologies from time to time, like web beacons or tracking pixels. These allow us to monitor traffic patterns and measure the success of our services. In many instances, these are reliant on essential identifiers to function properly.

How often will you update this Cookie Policy?

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the technologies we use or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Please revisit this policy regularly to stay informed.

Where can I get further information?

If you have any questions about our use of cookies or other technologies, please email us at chargeapp.support@gmail.com.
